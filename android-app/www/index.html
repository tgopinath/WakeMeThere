Trip simulator

Stands in for real GPS. Start a stop on the phone, then speed the trip up here.
  1. Tap Start next to a stop, or + to search for a new one. Swipe a stop left to edit or delete it. Past trips are under the Trips tab.
  2. Optionally Lock phone to see the background alert.
  3. Wait for the alarm, or press Skip ahead. Then try Snooze, or I'm awake to log the trip.
